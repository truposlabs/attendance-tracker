Privacy Policy

Application: Attendance Tracker (com.truposlabs.attendance)
Effective Date: 15 August 2026 | Last Updated: 15 August 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment

TruPosLabs ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Attendance Tracker mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards information on Android devices.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and handling of information as described herein, in compliance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Information Collection & Usage

A. Local Attendance & Leave Data

The App stores your attendance records, remote working status, booked leaves (Annual Leave, Sick/Carer's Leave, RDOs, Long Service Leave), custom holidays, and calendar preferences directly on your device using encrypted local SQLite storage.

📍 Location Data & Automated Office Check-In (Optional & Future Features)

In upcoming versions, Attendance Tracker may provide an optional feature to automate in-office check-ins using geofencing / location detection:

  • Purpose: Used strictly to verify arrival or departure at designated office coordinates.
  • Explicit Permission: Location access is 100% opt-in. You must explicitly grant system location permissions.
  • No Tracking or Selling: Real-time routes, location history, and travel movements are never logged, stored on remote servers, or shared with third parties.
  • Full Control: You can revoke location permissions at any time in Android Settings and continue using manual attendance logging.

B. No Advertising or Analytics Trackers

Attendance Tracker contains no third-party advertisements, behavioral profiling trackers, or marketing SDKs.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Calculating 50% hybrid in-office attendance compliance across the October–September cycle.
  • Forecasting required weekly pace, buffer days, and year-end projections.
  • Accurately deducting approved leaves and public holidays from working days.
  • Automating office presence verification when geofenced check-in is enabled by you.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or location data with third parties. All current data is stored strictly in your device's isolated application sandbox.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your records remain on your device as long as the application is installed. You can delete individual logs inside the app or completely remove all data by clearing App Storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.
